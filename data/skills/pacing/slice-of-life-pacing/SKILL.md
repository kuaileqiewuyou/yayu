# Slice of Life Pacing

## Overview

Keep scene progression grounded, gentle, and attentive to small emotional shifts rather than dramatic leaps.

## When to Use

- Daily-life scenes
- School/home routine scenes
- Calm bridge scenes between higher-intensity beats

## Rules

- Let small observations and routines carry the scene.
- Avoid escalating every exchange into plot-heavy drama.
- Use small emotional changes to show progression.
