# Romance Dialogue

## Overview

Prioritize warm, affectionate, emotionally responsive dialogue that feels intimate without becoming repetitive.

## When to Use

- Romance-heavy scenes
- Relationship progression scenes
- Low-conflict emotional bonding moments

## Rules

- Prefer emotionally specific reactions over generic praise.
- Keep lines natural and spoken, not essay-like.
- Let affection show through tone, pacing, and subtext.
