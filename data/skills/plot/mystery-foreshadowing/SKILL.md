# Mystery Foreshadowing

## Overview

Plant subtle clues and unanswered questions without over-explaining the plot too early.

## When to Use

- Mystery or suspense routes
- Investigation scenes
- Transitional scenes before major reveals

## Rules

- Prefer suggestive detail over explicit explanation.
- Leave at least one unresolved thread when advancing a mystery beat.
- Keep clues legible in hindsight, but not obvious immediately.
